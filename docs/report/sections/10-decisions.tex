\section{Design decisions changed during development}
\label{sec:changed}

The spec asks for the decisions we changed, with reasons, and the class notes ask us to document what happened and what we did. We kept a design log from the first day: every decision has an entry with its context, the options we rejected, the reasons and the consequences, and a changed decision gets a new entry while the old one is marked as amended or superseded, never rewritten (\dlog{D70}). There are 259 entries, numbered from D1 to D266. Table~\ref{tab:changed} lists the changes that matter most. Section~\ref{sec:raft} already covered the biggest one in full.

Two habits helped us here. First, every decision went through the same short procedure, written in \texttt{AGENTS.md}: read the design documents, ask about each open question with options and a recommendation, decide, record it, and update the architecture diagrams in the same change. Second, the lab results fed straight back into decisions. Most entries after \dlog{D230} start with a measurement from the six PCs, and say how the next run would check the change.
